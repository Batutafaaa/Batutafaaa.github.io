I am currently working as a Research Assistant at the Foundation for Agrarian Studies, Bangalore. As part of the **Digital Agriculture in India** project, my current research focuses on the agricultural drone market. I am mapping the agri-drone market's regulatory architecture, analyze its market dynamics, and identify the public and private actors shaping this emerging sector. We intend to understand how policies and technologies address the persistent concerns of equity, accessibility, and affordability in Indian agriculture.

My broader intellectual interest lie in political economy aspects of labour process, changing nature of welfare governance, and administration of natural resources in India.
