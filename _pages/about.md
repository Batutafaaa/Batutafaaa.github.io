Welcome! 

I am currently working as a Research Assistant at Foundation for Agrarian Studies on the **Digital Agriculture in India** project. Specifically, I am exploring the evolving ecosystem of agricultural drones in India by mapping the regulatory environment, analyzing the agricultural drone market, and identifying the key public and private actors involved.


My broader research interest involves understanding the dynamics of capitalist development, its impact on employment, governance and environmental conflicts. I aim to develop my understanding of political economy, and contribute towards understanding the functions of the state and its role in devlopment and shaping of markets in India. The works of Nicos Poulantzas, Bob Jessop, Jairus Banaji have inspired me to further investiagte literature in state theory and political economy. I am keen to devlop my skills in quantitative economic analysis to envision alternative governance models, inspired by the frameworks devoped by Leonid Kantorovich, A.R.G. Heesterman, Oskar Lange and Paul Cockshott.
